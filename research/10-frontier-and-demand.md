# What is genuinely unknown, and who actually wants it translated

## Verdict

- The “unknown text” romance is **half-true**. The analogue is not a sealed Sakya cache of 84,000 untranslated wonders. It is the gNas bcu lha khang at ’Bras spungs: catalogued 2004, published from 2006, works previously known only from rumour. That changed scholarship. It did not create a mass English-reading public.
- What remains *unstudied* (archives, medical/astro corpora, local histories, minor *rnam thar*, letters, *bca’ yig*) is larger than what remains *untranslated into English*. Scholars want editions and catalogues, not AI English of unknown manuals.
- Frontier work is already staffed (Paltsek, Hugon/Kano, van der Kuijp, Silk’s ERC BEST, Gyatso, Ramble, Divergent Discourses). None describes a vacant field waiting for an OSS repo.
- Demand is real but **segmented**. Practitioners want known dharma (Lotsawa House, 84000). Tibetologists want e-texts. AI labs want training data. Tibetan-language communities want OCR and dictionaries. Almost nobody asks for English of unnamed Sakya leaves.
- Failures (ACIP’s dead website, THL’s grant starvation, CompassionAI’s 2023 freeze, USAID’s 2025 digitization freeze) share modes: encoding lock-in, no paying users, founder/grant dependence.
- An OSS “translate the hidden 84,000” would shout into a void. An OSS that feeds BDRC/OpenPecha/Dharmamitra with OCR and catalogues would not.

## Findings

### 1. Real recoveries

**Dunhuang (1900–1908).** [FACT] Stein and Pelliot emptied Cave 17 of tens of thousands of MSS, mostly Chinese and Old Tibetan, sealed ~11th c. ([Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/dunhuang-1/)). [FACT] Old Tibetan Annals/Chronicle: years 650–764. [FACT] Non-Buddhist ritual and secular documents survive here, not in later canon (R.A. Stein, *Tibetica Antiqua V*). Changed: imperial history no longer rebuilt from 12th-c. chronicles.

**Sanskrit originals from Tibetan monasteries (1930s).** [FACT] Almost no Buddhist Sanskrit MSS survive in India; Tibet is the main 8th-c.+ treasury ([Kano](https://brill.com/downloadpdf/display/book/9789004307438/B9789004307438_005.pdf)). [FACT] Sāṅkṛtyāyana’s four trips (1929/30–1938); 1936 found 156 works at Sa skya, Ngor, Zhwa lu ([Jayaswal 1936](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.529727); [Steinkellner](https://www.oeaw.ac.at/fileadmin/Institute/IKGA/PDF/digitales/steinkellner_leaves.pdf)) — including Dharmakīrti’s *Pramāṇavārttika*. [FACT] Many MSS had belonged to Sa skya; some walled into images. Changed: Indology stopped “restoring” Sanskrit from Tibetan.

**Gathang Bumpa (2006).** [FACT] Lhokha *mchod rten* yielded Old Tibetan ritual booklets (*Gnag rabs* etc.), c. 900–1100, independent of Dunhuang (Bellezza, *RET* 29, 2014).

**Drepung / gNas bcu lha khang (1990s rediscovery; 2004 catalogue).** Closest analogue to the viral “hidden library.” [FACT] Paltsek’s two-volume, 2,483-page *’Bras spungs dpe rnying dkar chag* covers five Drepung libraries ([Ducher, *RET* 55, 2020](https://d1i1jdw69xsqx0.cloudfront.net/digitalhimalaya/collections/journals/ret/pdf/ret_55_06.pdf); [BDRC MW28949](https://library.bdrc.io/show/bdr:MW28949)). [FACT] Hugon: **24,295** gNas bcu entries, 23,135 from outside; ≥4,417 bundles implied, 1,833 found ([Hugon 2021:416–17](https://www.oeaw.ac.at/fileadmin/Institute/IKGA/PDF/digitales/Hugon_2021_Mapping_tshad_ma_works_Jackson_Festschrift_415-460_with_erratum.pdf)). [FACT] Editors liken the find to Dunhuang for pre-17th-c. Tibet ([BDRC](https://library.bdrc.io/show/bdr:PR1NCLK)). [CLAIM] BDRC: original holding >40,000 volumes, later dispersed. Many items were 17th-c. civil-war spoils, opened 1980s–90s.

**bKa’ gdams gsung ’bum (KDSB), 2006–2015.** [FACT] 4×30 = 120 volumes, 533 items, >160 thinkers (Hugon 2021:423). [FACT] ~80% of first-set titles from gNas bcu (Ducher, citing Karma bde legs). [FACT] Of 35 distinct early *tshad ma* works Hugon maps, five had been published before KDSB. Phya pa’s *Tshad ma yid kyi mun sel* survives in **one** Drepung MS and now anchors a subfield ([IKGA](https://www.oeaw.ac.at/ikga/forschung/tibetologie/materialien/phyapa)). Same cache: 120-vol. history/*rnam thar* series (Karma bde legs: 40% from the temple); Jo nang ~70%; large bKa’ brgyud and Kālacakra series (Ducher 2020). Changed: 11th–13th-c. scholasticism no longer reconstructed from Sa skya Paṇḍita’s polemics.

**van der Kuijp.** [FACT] Unknown early-14th-c. chronicle (*Journal of Tibetology* 7, 2011, [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/3668645)); Beijing CPN *tshad ma* MSS (1994); co-founded TBRC/BDRC 1999 ([Fairbank](https://fairbank.fas.harvard.edu/person/leonard-van-der-kuijp/)).

None of these is “English of a sealed library.” Sequence: catalogue → Tibetan facsimile → specialist edition. English last.

### 2. Still unstudied (not merely untranslated)

Rheingans (2015): *dris lan*, art genres, *za’ yig*, *gdan yig* “go almost unmentioned”; “vast number of unstudied texts” ([Brill](https://brill.com/display/book/9789004301153/9789004301153_webready_content_text.pdf)). [FACT] Schaeffer 2009 used “more than 150 understudied” sources.

| Category | Research-novel because | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Archives / *bca’ yig* | Ramble: archival literature “relatively little scholarly attention,” vital for law/tax/social history ([2018](https://uplopen.com/en/chapters/e/10.1515/9783110541397-003)). [FACT] ~2–3 million docs in TAR National Archives; Bonn digitized 2,700 Kundeling items. Jansen: most pre-1950s deeds destroyed. | Lhasa access vetted; Mustang/exile slices usable. |
| Medical | Schaeffer on Gyatso 2015: “Little of this literature has received attention… available… for a decade or so.” Gyatso: 12th–17th-c. texts “hitherto little studied, if at all.” | *Four Treatises* famous; commentarial ocean not. |
| *skar rtsis* / astro | IATS panels exist; van der Kuijp on Kangxi calendric translations. No field survey of unpublished *skar rtsis* found. | [SPECULATION] large and unread. |
| Local histories, letters, minor ritual | KDSB + 120-vol. history/*rnam thar* prove volume; Hugon: KDSB omitted listed bKa’ gdams works. | Philological novelty, not “lost wisdom.” |
| Women’s *rnam thar* | Schaeffer 2004: thousands of male bios vs “less than a handful” of female autobiographies. Ḍākinī Treasury (53 vols.) is the catch-up. | Academic + practitioner, not viral. |
| Dunhuang Sino-Tibetan | Silk ERC BEST (2025–29): prior work “largely impressionistic” ([CORDIS](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101141463)). | Already funded. |

[SPECULATION] Interesting predicates: unpublished in Tibetan, uncatalogued, or unedited — not “untranslated into English.”

### 3. Who is mining it, and what they say is left

- **Paltsek** (2002–): industrial publisher of recovered Central Tibetan MSS (Ducher 2020).
- **Hugon + Kano**, “Gateway to Early Tibetan Scholasticism” ([S2475](https://www.sakyaresearch.org/sources/2475)): KDSB catalogue; Hugon: **not all** Drepung bKa’ gdams works were included.
- **van der Kuijp**: recovered chronicles/*tshad ma*; TBRC; Norbu Ketaka NLP-corrected ~1 million OCR pages ([Fairbank](https://fairbank.fas.harvard.edu/research/blog/norbu-ketaka-tibetan-manuscripts/)).
- **Silk**, ERC BEST 2025–29 and Open Philology (ERC 741884, 2018–22): Dunhuang HTR and Chinese-into-Tibetan fingerprints. Not “hidden Sakya wisdom” — a polemical Indian-only origin story.
- **Schaeffer**: book culture; 2018 Lotsawa workshop with Gyatso: Tibetan literature as *literature* is “only beginning.”
- **Gyatso**: medical history from texts that became available in the 2000s.
- **Ramble**: Mustang archives; TAR National Archives mostly closed.
- **Divergent Discourses** (Barnett/Erhard/Hill; AHRC+DFG 2023–27): ~10,000 pages of 1955–62 Tibetan newspapers, HTR, NER ([project](https://research.uni-leipzig.de/diverge/)).

What they say is left: more MSS than readers; missing Drepung bundles; unedited KDSB items; Dunhuang still impressionistic; closed archives. None names “no English” as bottleneck.

### 4. Demand

**Practitioners.** [FACT] Lotsawa House (2004–): 6,000+ texts, nine languages; 345 new translations in 2025 ([about](https://www.lotsawahouse.org/about/)). [FACT] 84000 (2009–): Kangyur ~50% published / ~80% translated; Tengyur 0.7% published, goal 2110; 2026 claim of 100k unique Reading Room visitors/month ([progress](https://84000.co/progress-and-impact)). These are *known* texts. [FACT] 84000 2026: AI must **not** produce canonical translations. Khyentse Vision Project same line.

**Tibetologists.** IATS 2026: ~700 scholars, 39 countries; Nepal then cancelled in-person under Chinese pressure ([IATS](https://www.iats.info/2025/06/17th-iats-seminar-2026-call-for-submissions/)). A few hundred professionals, not a consumer market.

**Tibetan-language users.** Monlam ITRC (223-vol. dictionary, Melong.ai). Esukhia (2009–) spun out OpenPecha, Botok, OCR with BDRC. PRC labs ship OCR/LLMs for Potala and medical digitization ([Xinhua 2026-06-22](https://english.news.cn/20260622/ab6fb8633c6a418db6ded55aea36191a/c.html)). Demand is survival and search, not English.

**DH / AI.** [FACT] BDRC+Khyentse (Dec 2025–27): open Tibetan Buddhist AI datasets; 26M images OCR’d; Gold Standard XML 1.9→3.4 GB in two months; HuggingFace public domain ([BDRC](https://www.bdrc.io/blog/2026/02/28/bdrc-launches-major-initiative-to-build-open-buddhist-datasets-for-ai/)). Dharmamitra is production MT. r/tibetanlanguage (2024): label LLM output; prefer Monlam/Dharmamitra over ChatGPT ([thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/tibetanlanguage/comments/1ef0s33/use_of_machine_translations_in_rtibetanlanguage/)). McLeod 2023: GPT-4 mangled Garab Dorje ([Tricycle](https://tricycle.org/magazine/tibetan-ai-translation/)).

**Viral X.** [FACT] [@RjNol 2026-08-27](https://x.com/RjNol/status/2092806882981302740): “84,000… 95%… yet to be translated (and there is no real will).” ~1.5M views, ~32k likes. Curiosity, not a spec. No substantial Hacker News thread was found.

### 5. Attempts that died

| Project | What happened | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|
| **ACIP** (1987–) | Kangyur/Tengyur input; website later “dysfunctional”; volunteer Wylie mirror ([Steinert](https://www.christian-steinert.de/home/tibetan-texts/all-tibetan-texts-from-the-asian-classics-input-project-converted-to-wylie-transliteration)). Rehomed as Asian Legacy Library 2021 ([ALL](https://asianlegacylibrary.org/zh-hant/about-asian-legacy-library/history/)). | Encoding lock-in; founder-tied; site rot. |
| **THL** (UVa, 2000–) | Still at [thlib.org](https://thlib.org/); born with “no funding whatsoever” then grant bursts ([UVa wiki](https://collab.its.virginia.edu/wiki/aboutthl/THL%20History.html)). Tsadra 2009: after THDL→THL, “not being able to find half of the things” ([Tsadra](https://www.tsadra.org/update-on-the-tibetan-himalayan-library/)). | Grant sprawl; migration loss; DH rot. |
| **CompassionAI/lotsawa** | NLLB fine-tunes; last commit Oct 2023; 1 star ([repo](https://github.com/CompassionAI/lotsawa)). | Prototype, no institution. |
| **USAID scripture digitization** | ~$2M/year frozen 2025 ([WaPo](https://washingtonpost.com/world/2025/03/28/india-tibet-china-trump-foreign-aid-cuts)). | Single-donor politics. |

What lived: BDRC (from Gene Smith’s TBRC), 84000, Lotsawa House, Monlam, Esukhia/OpenPecha, Dharmamitra — endowed/sponsored, *known* corpus, Tibetan staff. There was never crowdsourced translation of *unknown* monastic MSS at scale.

### 6. Romance vs users

Romance is justified **as philology**: Drepung-style recoveries are real and unfinished. It is **not** justified as “95% of 84,000 Sakya texts untranslated.” That slogan collapses uncatalogued / unpublished-in-Tibetan / unpublished-in-English / unread-except-by-specialists. Paltsek 2006–15 already opened the main Central Tibetan cache. English is a downstream luxury. Demand for AI-assisted *known dharma* is real and crowded. Demand for English of genuinely unknown texts is a few dozen scholars who will not trust the English.

## Gaps and what would settle them

- Remainder of Paltsek recoveries still unedited, title by title. Next: Hugon/Kano Gateway vs KDSB dkar chag vs BDRC work IDs.
- Sakya’s present holdings vs 1930s notes vs the viral 84,000. Next: current Sa skya dkar chag / PRC census. **This file does not settle 84,000.**
- Unpublished *skar rtsis* and medical commentaries beyond Gyatso’s sample. Next: Men-Tsee-Khang and Paltsek medical inventories.
- Whether TAR’s 2–3 million archive documents open. Next: a PRC partnership; not an OSS problem.
- Monthly users of Dharmamitra/Monlam/OpenPecha CAT: public telemetry not found. H-Buddhism archive not fully searchable here; no HN thread found.

## Implication for the open-source project

- **Against** the tweet-shaped product. Twenty years of unknown-text excitement was *Tibetan publication of recovered MSS* (Paltsek), then specialist mining. English dumps would not be cited as evidence.
- **Against** competing with 84000 / Lotsawa House / Khyentse Vision on Kangyur, *gsung ’bum*, Dzogchen. That demand is funded; 84000 rejects AI as translator of record.
- **For** infrastructure frontier labs already lack: work-level catalogues of recovered collections, HTR for *dbu med*, parallel-sentence mines, public-domain e-texts (BDRC’s 2026 grant is building this too). Feeder, not cathedral.
- **For** Tibetan-language (not English) tools: OCR, search, NER, dictionary, speech — Monlam/Esukhia/OpenPecha/BDRC.
- **Neutral-to-against** on “unknown administrative records in English.” Wanted form is a diplomatic edition.
- **Failure-mode constraints:** no new encoding; no single grant/founder; no rotting website (ACIP, THL); no canonical-authority claim. If the repo cannot name an existing consumer (BDRC, Dharmamitra, Hugon, Monlam), it is the void.
