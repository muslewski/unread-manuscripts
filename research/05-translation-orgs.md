# Existing translation efforts and how much is genuinely untranslated

## Verdict

- [FACT] The Kangyur is **not** untranslated. 84000’s April 2026 milestone: **50% published in English, ~80% already translated**. Target **100% by 2035**. Tengyur **is** in scope and almost untouched: **~0.7% published, ~5% in pipeline**, target **2110**. ([progress](https://84000.co/progress-and-impact); [29 Apr 2026](https://www.facebook.com/Translate84000/posts/with-deep-gratitude-84000-is-celebrating-a-significant-milestone50-of-the-kangyu/1375908674563507/))
- [FACT] A dense professional ecosystem already exists (84000, Tsadra, Lotsawa House, Padmakara, Dharmachakra, KVP, LOTC, AIBS, Khenpo Appey Foundation). The gap is **not** “nobody is translating Tibetan.”
- [FACT] Sakya literature is **selectively** translated (Lamdré core, Sapaṇ, Gorampa’s *Distinguishing the Views*, Ngorchen Könchog Lhündrup’s *Three Visions*). The **Sakya Kabum** as a set is barely in English. Much Lamdré/Hevajra is **restricted**; open publication is lineage-gated.
- [FACT] “Less than 5% translated” refers to the **Kangyur+Tengyur canon as of ~2009**, not “all Tibetan writing,” and is **stale for the Kangyur**. [SPECULATION] Indigenous literature is still overwhelmingly untranslated — likely **well under a few percent** of BDRC’s ~25 million Tibetan pages. No scholar publishes a trustworthy percentage for that denominator.
- [FACT] Major orgs **reject unsupervised outside AI** of canonical or tantric texts. 84000 forbids AI first drafts. Tsadra, KVP, and Monlam/MITRA want AI as **assistive tooling**. A scrape-and-LLM English dump would fight the incumbents and violate tantric restriction.

## Findings

### 1. 84000

**Dashboard** (indexed from [84000.co/progress-and-impact](https://84000.co/progress-and-impact) on **11 May 2026**; live counters are JS widgets and did not extract via HTTP fetch on 27 Aug 2026). [FACT]

| Collection | Published | Awaiting pub. | In progress | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kangyur (~70,000 Peking-side pp; 1,169 texts) | 32,632 (**50%**) | 18,816 (**29%**) | 12,002 (**19%**) | **100% by 2035** |
| Tengyur (~161,800 pp; 4,093 texts) | 859 (**0.7%**) | 1,359 (**1%**) | 3,699 (**3%**) | **100% by 2110** |

Published + awaiting ≈ 79% of Kangyur, matching Dzongsar Khyentse’s 29 Apr 2026 “~80% translated / 50% available” ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=flR78NySlIo); [BDG 22 May 2026](https://www.buddhistdoor.net/news/online-dharma-buddhist-translation-project-84000-announces-branding-and-website-refresh/)). Kangyur not-started ~2%. Tengyur **~95% not started**. Pages are **Peking-side** equivalents ([facts](https://84000.co/facts-and-figures-about-kangyur-and-tengyur); Stanley census). Combined canon ≈ **231,800 pages**.

**Funding.** [FACT] KF seed: **108 founding sponsors / US$5M** after the 2009 Bir conference ([KF history](https://khyentsefoundation.org/story/translating-the-words-of-the-buddha/)). 501(c)(3) since 2013. Grants: **>US$8.5M, >165 grants, >60 groups** ([grants](https://84000.co/grants-and-opportunities)). 2024: **$2.19M raised**, 4,722 pp / 74 texts published, Kangyur **42%** by year-end ([annual reports](https://84000.co/about/annual-reports); [2024 PDF](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6984c8620f76e006500a539e/69ebe407188bff2684956ce8_84000-Annual-Report-2024-Distribution-Eng.pdf)). Form 990 FY2024: revenue **$2.79M**, expenses **$3.68M**, assets **$14.2M** ([ProPublica](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/454302237)). 2023: **>$3M** including a **$1M** crowdfund.

**Tengyur** is the 100-year second phase. First Tengyur publication: 2022. Kangyur grants **closed**; Tengyur round **late 2027**; translators **experts only** ([FAQ](https://84000.co/faq)). Not a volunteer Wikipedia.

### 2. Other organized efforts

[FACT] Volumes are what the orgs themselves publish.

- **Lotsawa House** ([about](https://www.lotsawahouse.org/about/)): free library since 2004; **>6,000 texts**, 9 languages. Projects: Ḍākinī Treasury, JKCL sungbum (500 EN texts 2019–23), Longchen Nyingtik, Nyingtik Yabzhi. Sakya: [Kabum series](https://www.lotsawahouse.org/topics/sakya-kabum/) = **15 English texts** from a **15-volume** set. Explicit restricted-text policy.
- **Tsadra** ([translation](https://www.tsadra.org/translation/)): EN/FR funding; Kongtrul DNZ (18 vols); Nyingma Tradition; Buddha-Nature wiki. **508** Tibetan titles / **34,811** Tib. pages; **104** EN books. Sakya: DNZ vols 5–6 (Malcolm Smith). Rimé, not Sakya-first.
- **Padmakara** (1987; [profile](https://www.tsadra.org/translators/padmakara-translation-group/)): Nyingma/Kagyu classics. **No Sakya program.**
- **Dharmachakra** (2006, Chökyi Nyima; [profile](https://www.tsadra.org/translators/dharmachakra-translation-committee/)): 13 Great Scriptures + Mipham/Shenga; major 84000 contractor. Canonical, not Sakya-school.
- **Khyentse Foundation**: incubator, not a desk. Spawned 84000, [Khyentse Vision Project](https://www.khyentsevision.org/) (Khyentse Wangpo kabum + CAT/AI studio), Kumarajiva (Chinese). KVP is Khyentse-lineage, not Sakya Kabum.
- **Nitartha** ([profile](https://www.tsadra.org/translators/nitartha-institute/)): Kagyu/Nyingma shedra textbooks. **No Sakya.**
- **Esukhia** ([digital](https://esukhia.net/digital-resources)): **etexts/NLP**, not EN translation. Degé Kangyur/Tengyur Unicode (GitHub, CC0); partners BDRC, Monlam, Berkeley.
- **Light of Berotsana** ([berotsana.org](https://www.berotsana.org/)): Nyingma/Dzogchen; many **restricted** publications. **No Sakya focus.**
- **Tibetan Classics Translators Guild of NY**: Geshe Lozang Jamspal et al.; 84000 Kangyur grantee ([list](https://84000.co/grants-and-opportunities)). Not Sakya-school.
- **Library of Tibetan Classics** (Jinpa / Wisdom): curated **32 vols**, ~200 texts; **~20 published or in press** by 2026. Not a complete canon. Sakya by design: vol. 4 Lamdré (Stearns); vol. 10 includes Sapaṇ; vol. 20 Sakya epistemology (Kapstein, forthcoming) ([Wisdom](https://wisdomexperience.org/library-of-tibetan-classics/)).
- **AIBS / Treasury of Buddhist Sciences** (Columbia): slow academic Tengyur series, predates 84000 ([aibs.columbia.edu](https://aibs.columbia.edu/)).
- **Khenpo Appey Foundation**: dedicated **Sakya** series at Wisdom — Gorampa *Light of Samantabhadra* (Kilty); *Illuminating Samsara and Nirvana* (Toh, listed Dec 2026); other drafts ([KAF](https://www.khenpoappey.org/en/translation-projects)). Output is still a handful of books.

These groups share funders (Tsadra, KF) and people. They curate; they do not exhaust indigenous corpora.

### 3. Sakya-specific translation and the restriction wall

**Sakya Kabum.** [FACT] Derge/Dehra Dun = **15 vols** ([BDRC MW3KG755](https://library.bdrc.io/show/bdr:MW3KG755)); later comparative edition **26 vols** ([MW3CN485](https://library.bdrc.io/show/bdr:MW3CN485)). Lotsawa House: **15 texts**, not 15 volumes. No complete English Kabum. [CLAIM] A 2025 Zenodo “AI Translation Series” dump of “Sakya Collected Works” is machine output, not a scholarly edition.

**Lamdré.** [FACT] First major English compilation: Cyrus Stearns, *Taking the Result as the Path* (LOTC 4, 2006), with H.H. Sakya Trizin’s approval. Publisher: most of these writings “traditionally have been considered secret.” Preliminaries are more open: Ngorchen Könchog Lhündrup, *Three Visions* (Dagpa & Goldberg, 1991/2002); Dezhung Rinpoche, *Three Levels of Spiritual Perception* (Wisdom, 1995). Tibetan Lamdré sets (tsogs bshad, slob bshad) run many volumes on BDRC (W23648, W23649) and are **not** translated as sets. [CLAIM] Lama Choedak (1997) said Hevajra initiation is still capped at **≤25** disciples ([PDF](https://www.buddhanet.net/pdf_file/lamdre.pdf) — old, secondary).

**Sakya Paṇḍita.** [FACT] *Clarifying the Sage’s Intent* in LOTC 10 (Jackson). *Jewel Treasury of Wise Sayings* has multiple English versions. *Treasury of Reasoning* awaits LOTC vol. 20 (Kapstein, forthcoming). Collected works not in English.

**Gorampa.** [FACT] 13 recovered volumes reprinted at Derge from 1905 ([SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/gorampa)). English: Cabezón & Dargyay, *Freedom from Extremes* (2007); Kilty, *Light of Samantabhadra* (partial); Toh, *Illuminating Samsara and Nirvana* (announced 2026). A **tiny** fraction of the sungbum.

**Ngorchen.** Distinguish **Künga Zangpo** (Ngor founder) from **Könchog Lhündrup** (10th abbot). [FACT] The latter’s *Three Visions* is the standard English Lamdré-preliminary. The founder’s works are not systematically in English.

**Restriction is a hard block.** [FACT] Lotsawa House: some texts “only for those who have received the requisite empowerments, transmissions and instructions” ([policy](https://www.lotsawahouse.org/about/restricted-texts-policy)). Berotsana sells “Restricted Material.” KVP: majority of its texts need *lung*, *wang*, *tri*; “AI cannot receive or transmit these prerequisites”; deciding what to restrict is human-only ([Tech & A.I.](https://www.khyentsevision.org/tech-ai-development/)). An open GitHub of raw Lamdré English is a **lineage violation**, not a public good.

### 4. The honest denominator

**Canon, ~231,800 pages.** [FACT] 2009 KF/84000 baseline: **<5% of the canon** in any modern language ([KF](https://khyentsefoundation.org/kf-projects/84000/)). 84000 claimed **12%** by Dec 2021 ([press](https://84000.co/news-event/press-release-worlds-first-english-catalog-of-the-collected-treatises-of-the-ancient-buddhist-world-now-complete)). May 2026 arithmetic: **published** 32,632+859 = **33,491 pp ≈ 14% of the canon**; Kangyur translated-or-awaiting (~51k) plus Tengyur pipeline (~6k) ≈ **25% of the canon in some translated state**, almost all Kangyur. Tengyur remains ~**99% unpublished**.

**Indigenous literature.** [FACT] BDRC: **>30 million** scanned pages, **~25 million Tibetan** (Ronis, 2024; [KF](https://khyentsefoundation.org/story/the-buddhist-digital-resource-center-celebrates-25-years-of-preserving-buddhist-literature/)). Includes 20 Kangyurs, 6 Tengyurs, and sungbum of **600+** masters ([BDRC 2022](https://www.bdrc.io/blog/2022/01/06/dharma-jewels-online-the-bdrc-collections/)). Tsadra’s entire funded output: **34,811** Tibetan pages. Lotsawa House’s 6,000 items are mostly **short** texts.

**No scholarly % exists** for “all Tibetan writing in a European language.” The 5% figure is routinely misapplied to “Tibetan literature” or “Sakya’s 84,000 manuscripts.” [SPECULATION] Even 200,000 published European pages of *indigenous* material would be **<1% of 25 million**. Honest line: **Kangyur English is now majority-translated; Tengyur and indigenous writing are not.**

### 5. Do these orgs want outside AI help?

**84000 — no to MT-as-translator.** [FACT] Policy: [84000 and AI](https://84000.co/documentation/84000s-position-on-ai-and-the-machine-translation-of-canonical-literature). No AI first drafts of canonical texts. Allowed: variant comparison, glossary drafts, QA, alignment, TM. Forbidden: unsupervised MT even of unpublished texts; letting the model pick interpretations. Huang, May 2026: AI may “aid our scholars”; “human stewardship will always be at the heart” ([BDG](https://www.buddhistdoor.net/news/online-dharma-buddhist-translation-project-84000-announces-branding-and-website-refresh/)). Grants: Kangyur closed; Tengyur 2027; experts only. Not an OSS volunteer funnel.

**Tsadra — tooling, not replacement.** [FACT] Three-year Dharmamitra collab (Nehrdich, Keutzer, BAIR). AI is “not a replacement for human translators” ([AI tools](https://www.tsadra.org/ai-tools-for-tibetan-buddhist-translation/)).

**KVP — same, plus they build the tools.** [FACT] No full MT for publication; line-by-line review with a lineage scholar; *Loshimi* CAT+agents; they released an alignment model on Hugging Face. They ask for **collaboration on tooling**, not competing corpora ([Tech & A.I.](https://www.khyentsevision.org/tech-ai-development/)).

**Monlam AI — yes, and they already ship MT.** [FACT] Launched Nov 2023, demoed to the Dalai Lama ([CTA](https://staging.tibet.net/sikyong-penpa-tsering-attends-launch-of-first-ever-tibetan-ai-tool-developed-by-monlam-it-research-centre/); [RFA](https://www.rfa.org/english/news/tibet/translation-software-11152023122728.html)). MITRA fine-tuned on **~2M** Tib–EN pairs from Monlam ([Cambridge](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/bitstreams/ef9540b5-cd53-4297-86ce-692ba871f643/download)). This is the open MT stack. It is **not** 84000’s publication pipeline.

**Incumbent consensus:** CAT, alignment, OCR, glossary, QA — yes. Outside OSS that **publishes raw LLM English of Kangyur, Tengyur, or restricted tantra** — no.

## Gaps and what would settle them

- **Live 84000 counters on 27 Aug 2026.** Fetch missed the widgets. Settle: screenshot, or a 2025 annual report (not posted).
- **Exact % of indigenous literature in any European language.** No census. Settle: join BDRC work-IDs to Tsadra/Wisdom/Shambhala/academic bibliographies and count pages.
- **Page-exact Kabum and Gorampa coverage.** Lotsawa House lists texts, not pages. Settle: Derge volume/folio → published English table.
- **What restricted Lamdré 84000/LOTC/KAF will never publish.** Only lineage offices know. Settle: ask the Sakya Trizin / Ngor / Tsar offices for a licensable list.
- **Whether 84000 or Tsadra would take an external CAT/OCR plugin.** Public pages name in-house or partner tools only. Settle: a direct partnership inquiry, not a GitHub dump.

## Implication for the open-source project

- **AGAINST** “translate the 84,000 / translate the canon with AI.” The Kangyur is a staffed, funded, 50%-published project with a 2035 deadline and a ban on AI first drafts. Competing with 84000 on Kangyur English is duplication they will not accept.
- **NEUTRAL-TO-AGAINST** on Tengyur. The unmet fraction is real (~99% unpublished), but 84000 has claimed it through 2110 and opens expert grants in 2027. An OSS AI Tengyur would be a parallel, lower-status corpus unless they adopted the tooling.
- **FOR, narrowly, on exoteric indigenous Sakya** (Madhyamaka, pramāṇa, history, vinaya, *lekshé*): Gorampa, Sapaṇ’s non-tantric works, shedra textbooks. KAF is the incumbent and is slow. A **human-in-the-loop** pipeline that feeds KAF/Tsadra/Wisdom is the only non-hostile design.
- **AGAINST open publication of Lamdré, Hevajra, and other restricted tantra**, AI or not. The tradition blocks it.
- **FOR tooling, AGAINST a translation brand.** OCR, etext cleanup, alignment, CAT plugins, glossary sharing — that is what 84000/Tsadra/KVP say they will use. Monlam/MITRA already occupy raw MT. The non-duplicative slice is **Sakya bibliographic mapping + assistive tools offered to incumbents**, not a new English Kangyur.
